This is the one that convinces people who think they do not like mushrooms.
Coarsely crushed black pepper, curry leaves bloomed in coconut oil, and a fry
taken far enough that the edges go properly crisp.

It is a *dry* fry. There should be no sauce at the end — just seasoning clinging
to the mushrooms.

## Get the pepper right

Crush whole peppercorns coarsely in a mortar just before cooking. Pre-ground
pepper has lost the volatile oils that make this dish work, and the texture of
coarse crush against a crisp edge is half the point.

## Serving

With rice and rasam, inside a dosa, or on its own with a wedge of lime.

| Heat level | Adjust |
| --- | --- |
| Milder | 1 tsp pepper, 1 chilli |
| As written | 1½ tsp pepper, 2 chillies |
| Hotter | 2 tsp pepper, 3 chillies, add a slit green chilli |
