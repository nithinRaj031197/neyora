Oyster mushrooms fry better than almost any vegetable. The torn edges hold
batter, and the flesh stays juicy while the outside goes glassy and crisp.

The crust here is rice flour and cornflour, no wheat, which gives a lighter and
longer-lasting crispness than a plain flour batter.

## The one thing that ruins this

Wet mushrooms. Water in the batter turns to steam, steam softens the crust, and
you get a soggy shell that slides off. Brush them clean and keep them dry.

## Oil temperature

175–180 °C. Below that they absorb oil; above and the crust colours before the
inside is done. No thermometer? Drop in a pinch of batter — it should sink
briefly, then rise and sizzle steadily.

> A rack, not a plate lined with kitchen paper. Paper traps steam under the
> pieces and softens the base within a minute.
