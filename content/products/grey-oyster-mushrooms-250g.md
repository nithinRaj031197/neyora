Grey oyster mushrooms (*Pleurotus ostreatus*) grown on the same pasteurised
sawdust biomass pellets as the white, in the same room, to the same routine.

The difference is in the eating. The grey has a denser, slightly chewier bite
and a deeper savoury note — it holds its shape better in a long braise, where
the white can soften. If you are frying hard and want the pieces to stay
distinct, this is the one to ask for.
