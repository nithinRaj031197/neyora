## Who we are

NEYORA. Our contact details are on the [contact page](/contact).

## What we collect

**When you use the website.** Nothing that identifies you. This site stores no
personal data of its own: there is no database, no account system and no
tracking script. We do not record your IP address or build a profile of you.

**When you contact us.** If you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is
governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. If you email us, we keep the message
in our mailbox so that we can reply.

## What we do not do

- Sell or rent your personal data to anyone
- Share it with advertisers or data brokers
- Use it for automated decision-making or profiling
- Send you marketing you did not ask for

## Cookies

We set no advertising or tracking cookies. See the [cookie policy](/cookies) for
the full detail.

## Who processes data for us

| Processor | Purpose | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudflare | Website hosting and delivery | Global edge network |

Cloudflare acts as a processor on our instructions, and keeps short-lived
operational logs as part of serving the site.

## Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of any personal data we hold about you, ask us to
correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the email address on our
[contact page](/contact) and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their
data.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
