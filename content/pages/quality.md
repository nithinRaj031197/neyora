Quality without compromise is easy to print and hard to do. Here is what it
actually means in our operation.

## What we test

| What | How often | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water | Quarterly, external lab | Substrate hydration and misting both use it |
| Substrate moisture | Every batch | Too wet invites bacteria, too dry stalls colonisation |
| Pasteurisation temperature | Every batch, logged | The single biggest contamination control |
| Room CO₂ and humidity | Continuously | Determines cluster shape and shelf life |
| Cold-chain temperature | Every dispatch | Texture is lost here, not on the farm |

## What we reject

A cluster does not go into a pack if it has a torn or dried cap edge, an off or
sour scent, visible contamination anywhere on the block, or a stem that has gone
woody. Between 8% and 12% of each harvest is diverted to compost. That number is
a feature, not a loss.

## What we do not use

- Pesticides or fungicides at any stage
- Growth regulators
- Post-harvest washing or chemical treatment
- Preservatives of any kind

## What we will not claim

We will not tell you mushrooms cure anything. Oyster mushrooms are a genuinely
good food — high in protein for a vegetable, high in fibre, low in calories, a
real source of potassium and B vitamins. That is a sound reason to eat them
often. It is not medicine, and we will not dress it up as medicine.

If we ever publish a nutrition figure, it will come from a lab report we can
show you.

## Traceability

Every pack can be traced to its harvest date, growing room, substrate batch and
spawn batch. Send us a harvest date and we will tell you exactly where it came
from.
