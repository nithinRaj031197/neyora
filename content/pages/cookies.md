## The short version

**This website sets no cookies.** None at all — not for advertising, not for
analytics, not for sessions. That is why you are not seeing a consent banner:
there is nothing to consent to.

## Why there are none

The site has no accounts and no database. Every page is built ahead of time from
files, so there is no session to remember and no visitor to identify.

## Analytics

We currently run no analytics on this site.

If we add any later it will be a privacy-friendly, cookie-free tool that counts
page views without identifying anyone, and this page will be updated to say so
before it goes live.

## Cookies set by other sites

If you follow a link to Instagram, YouTube or WhatsApp, that service may set its
own cookies once you are there. We do not embed their tracking scripts on this
site, so nothing is set until you leave.

## Controlling cookies

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies in its settings.
Blocking cookies will not affect your use of this website in any way.
