NEYORA grows food. That is the whole of it.

We began with grey oyster mushrooms for a specific reason: mushrooms are
unforgiving. There is no long ripening window to hide behind, no waxing, no
cold-chain trick that makes a three-day-old mushroom taste like a fresh one.
Either you picked it this morning and moved it quickly, or you did not — and
anyone who cooks can tell.

Starting somewhere unforgiving forced us to build the habits we wanted first:
harvest to order, cool immediately, grade by hand, print the harvest date, and
answer honestly when someone asks how it was grown.

## Why "GROWN FOR LIFE."

Three readings, all intended.

Grown **for life** — food that supports a healthy one.
Grown **for a lifetime** — a farm built to still be here in twenty years.
Grown **for living things** — soil, growers, and the people who eat it.

## What comes next

NEYORA is not a mushroom company. Mushrooms are our first crop. Fresh produce,
herbs and other farm products will follow, and every one of them will meet the
same standard: harvested to order, graded by hand, dated on the pack, and
explained plainly.

## What we will not do

- Sell produce we would not cook at home that evening
- Make a health claim we cannot support with a document
- Print a certification badge we have not earned
- Grow volume at the cost of the thing people buy us for
